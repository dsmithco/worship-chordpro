# Copy brief: Worship ChordPro, minimal pass

Dan read the first draft and said the site must be "very minimal and no
mansplaining text, minimal and intuitive and modern", like his Worship Sets
app, whose screens carry a title, controls and almost no sentences. Cut every
piece to the least that still does its job. The site is a searchable library
of worship songs in ChordPro (chord charts). Public domain hymns show words
and chords. Copyrighted songs show only title, writers, year and CCLI song
number, with a button to SongSelect, where a church with a license gets the
chart. Personal project of Dan Smith; never mention Gray Sand.

Dan's words the site must still carry, whole: "this site does not give
copyright to its visitors" and "appropriate license and permission is always
required before displaying printing or sharing lyrics from copy protected
work." Tidy grammar only.

Facts for the Copyright page: US public domain line is 1930 in 2026 (works
published 1930 or earlier); other countries count differently. Chord settings
on public domain hymns are free to use. A CCLI Church Copyright License covers
a church's own congregational use of songs in the CCLI catalog; it does not
let a website publish them, and streaming needs a separate license. The build
refuses lyrics for licensed songs and public domain claims after 1930. Not
legal advice. Report a problem: open an issue or a pull request at
github.com/dsmithco/worship-chordpro.

Return each piece under its marker, nothing before the first marker, no dashes.
