## Public domain
Works published in 1930 or earlier are in the US public domain in 2026. Other countries count differently. Chord settings on these hymns are free to use.

## Licensed songs
CCLI, short for Christian Copyright Licensing International, sells a Church Copyright License for a church's own use of songs in its catalog. It does not let a website publish them. Streaming needs a separate license. Licensed songs here show the title, writers, year, and CCLI song number, with a link to SongSelect. The site build refuses lyrics for licensed songs and public domain claims after 1930.
