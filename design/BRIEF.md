# Worship ChordPro: brief

Personal project, not client work. Dan asked on 2026-11-13 for a GitHub repo
of worship songs in ChordPro, a folder per letter and a folder per song with
its versions inside, searchable and filterable on a GitHub Pages site under
dsmithco, like a guitar tab site but for songs sung in church. It must say
plainly that the site gives visitors no rights in anyone's songs and that a
license and permission are needed before showing, printing or sharing
copyrighted lyrics.

## Who comes and why

- A worship leader or volunteer at rehearsal, phone on a music stand, who
  needs a song's chords in the key the singer wants, now.
- Church office staff who print or project words and need to know what they
  may use.
- A musician adding a song or a correction through GitHub.

## The site's one job

Find a song and read its chart. Second job: know at a glance which songs
carry a chart and which only link out to a licensed source.

## Feel and not-feel

Feel: the index at the back of a hymnal, a cloth cover with gilt lettering,
chords set in red the way a missal sets its rubrics. A tool you trust on a
dim stage. Not: a church website, not a SaaS landing page, not a tab site
plastered with ads.

## Facts

- 30 public domain hymns with full charts (31 files, When I Survey has two
  tunes) and 50 copyrighted songs listed with writers, year and CCLI number.
- US public domain line is 1930 in 2026; the build refuses anything later.
- ChordPro: chords in square brackets inside the lyric line, directives in
  braces.

## The sentence only this site could carry

Every hymn here is filed the way a hymnal files it, by first line and by
tune name in small caps, and the chords are set in red over the syllable
they fall on.

## System

- Type: Supreme (Fontshare, ITF) for titles, labels and chords; Sentient
  (Fontshare, ITF) for lyrics, first lines and running text.
- Color: near-white page, hymnal cloth navy oklch(0.27 0.075 262) for the
  cover band, the thumb tabs and the letters, gilt oklch(0.84 0.1 85) for
  the wordmark and tab letters, rubric red oklch(0.5 0.19 27) for chords and
  the word Chart. Dark mode is stage mode: navy page, warm white ink.
- Radius 2px. Hairline rules. No cards, no shadows.
- Signature: a thumb index down the right edge on desktop, cut tabs like a
  hymnal's fore-edge, the current letter's tab pulled out as the list
  scrolls; big sticky letters beside each group. Tapping a song carries its
  title into the page heading (cross-document view transition).
- No photography on purpose: the content is type, and the page should load
  instantly at rehearsal.

## Process notes

Stages 2 and 3 of the method (live reference board, three directions) were
skipped for this first build; the direction came straight from the hymnal
concept. The critique runs without a board.
